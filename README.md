Sum up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image. This entry is a newspaper front page drawn with Python: Nvidia's Huang returns as CEO, Russian strikes hit the Kyiv region as Ukraine sinks a warship off Libya, COP31 collapses with no deal, and Democrats win their biggest House majority since 1992 as storms threaten Thanksgiving travel.

## What it shows

A 1024x1024 front page, "THE WORLD IN ONE PAGE", dated TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER 2026. The lead story is Nvidia: the board reinstates Jensen Huang after President Trump intervenes, and shares jump 33%. Below it, three stories in a grid: the war in Ukraine, the COP31 climate talks in Antalya, and the United States. `draw_news.py` paints the page with Pillow and DejaVu fonts; `main.py` copies the finished `entry.png` to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
